Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

WealthPulse is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your financial information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

WealthPulse stores all data locally on your device. We collect:

  • Transaction records you manually enter (amounts, categories, dates)
  • Budget settings and spending limits
  • Financial goals and savings targets
  • App preferences and display settings

Financial Data: We do NOT connect to bank accounts, credit cards, or any external financial institutions. All financial data is entered manually by you and stored locally on your device.

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

All information collected is used solely for:

  • Providing personal finance tracking and budgeting features
  • Generating financial reports and insights
  • Setting and tracking savings goals
  • Improving app functionality and user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using secure iOS storage mechanisms.

We do not transmit your financial data to any third parties. Your transaction history, budgets, and financial goals never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

4. Subscription & Payments

Premium subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device until you:

  • Delete the WealthPulse app
  • Request data deletion through our support channels

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: View all your stored financial data within the app
  • Delete: Remove individual transactions or all app data at any time
  • Export: Export your data in standard formats for backup

7. Children's Privacy

WealthPulse is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

WealthPulse does not contain advertising, analytics trackers, or third-party services that collect user data. Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
